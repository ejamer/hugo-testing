**Website Privacy Notice**

This notice explains what information this website (fenb.ca) collects when you visit, and why. It covers the website only. How FENB handles members' personal information is set out in the [Privacy and Data Protection Policy](../privacy-data-protection/).

**Visit statistics (Google Analytics)**  
We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are most useful, so we can improve the site. It records the pages you view, the site that sent you here, your type of device and browser, and your approximate location (city or region). It doesn't collect your name, your email address, or anything you type on the site, and Google Analytics doesn't store IP addresses. It saves cookies in your browser to recognize repeat visits.

We use this information only as statistics. We don't use it for advertising, and we don't sell or share it. Google processes it for us under its own [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Opting out**

- If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal, Google Analytics doesn't record your visit and no analytics cookies are saved. Your browser still downloads Google's script file, but nothing is sent back.
- You can also block analytics cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's [opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

The site works the same either way.

**Club map**  
The Clubs page shows an embedded Google Map. When it loads, Google may set its own cookies under its privacy policy.

**What stays on your device**

- Your light or dark theme choice is saved in your browser only. It's never sent to us.
- Site search runs in your browser. Your searches aren't sent to us or anyone else.

**Contact form**  
The contact form opens your own email program to send your message. The site doesn't store it.

**Questions**  
Send questions about this notice or your information to [fencingnb@gmail.com](mailto:fencingnb@gmail.com).
