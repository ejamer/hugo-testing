Fencing NB is delighted to announce that **Jean-Marc Sinnasse** joined us as our new Executive Director in October 2026. We're excited about the skills and experience he brings to the role. His background in budgeting, funding, and building strong organizations is exactly what we need as we keep supporting our clubs and growing our sport across the province.

Jean-Marc first discovered fencing as a child, and more recently he has coached badminton and started introductory sessions for adult beginners. In his own words:

> "Hi everyone! Originally from France, I have been living in Summerside, Prince Edward Island, since January 2022. I bring over 25 years of experience in administrative and financial management, as well as team leadership, notably with the City of Paris and as Director of Support Services at Collège de l'Île. Currently working as a language monitor and mentor for the Department of Education, I am also a lifelong sports enthusiast and an avid badminton player. I look forward to working together to support and grow fencing across New Brunswick!"

Please join us in giving Jean-Marc a warm welcome to the New Brunswick fencing community.

We also want to extend our sincere thanks to our previous Executive Director, **Kathryn Howe**, for her years of excellent service in the role. Kathryn's dedication helped build a stronger, more connected fencing community in New Brunswick, and her steady work behind the scenes supported countless competitions, programs and initiatives. We are especially grateful for her continued support of the coaches, athletes and officials who make our sport what it is. Thank you, Kathryn. We wish you all the best!
