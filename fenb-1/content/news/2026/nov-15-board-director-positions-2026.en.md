Fencing-Escrime NB (FENB) is led by a volunteer Board of Directors, and we're looking for people to join it. **Three Director at Large positions** are open and will be filled by election at our **Annual General Meeting on December 6, 2026**.

If fencing has meant a lot to you or to someone you care about, serving on the Board is a powerful way to give back and help the sport keep thriving here in New Brunswick.

As a board member, you will:

- Help set policy and shape FENB's future direction
- Attend monthly virtual board meetings
- Support the work of FENB's committees

Everyone is welcome to apply. Fencing knowledge is useful but not required. Directors must be registered members of FENB in good standing (you can [renew or update your membership](https://2mev.com/#!/memberships/fencing-nb-2026-2027) online) and are elected for two-year terms. New directors will join returning board members David Collins, Céline Fournet (President), Colleen Parker and Gilles Robichaud.

**To apply:** fill in the [Board Application Form (PDF)](/documents/about/board-application-form-2026.pdf) and send it to [fencingnb@gmail.com](mailto:fencingnb@gmail.com) by **December 4, 2026**. For questions, contact FENB President Céline Fournet at the same address.

## Annual General Meeting 2026

- **Date:** Sunday, December 6, 2026
- **Time:** 1:00 – 3:00 PM
- **Location:** Escrime La Résistance Fencing, 123 Halifax Street, Moncton, NB

All FENB members are invited to attend. The Board will review the past year, hold Board elections and give updates on the organization.

**Voting:** every member registered for the 2026–2027 season, including Board members, has one vote. For members under 13 on the day of the meeting, a parent or guardian votes; members aged 13 to 15 may vote themselves or give their vote to a parent or guardian. If you can't attend, you can assign your vote to another member (a proxy) with a dated, signed letter. Each member may hold up to three proxy votes.

**Agenda**

1. Welcome from the FENB President
2. Acceptance of the agenda
3. Acceptance of the 2025 AGM minutes
4. Board of Directors' report
5. 2025–2026 financial report
6. Approval of accountants for 2026–2027
7. Election of directors
8. New business
9. Adjournment

The financial statements are still pending and will be sent to members once available. They will be marked "Draft" but will have been approved by the Board.
