Three New Brunswick fencers took their skills across the border to compete at the Ultimate Cup RJCC, a USA Fencing Regional Junior & Cadet Circuit event held October 31, 2026 in Hillsborough Township, New Jersey. Sammy Singh Ranger, Ethan Han and Bhikki Singh Ranger all competed in Cadet Men's Épée.

Congratulations to all three on representing New Brunswick on the international stage! Two of them fenced their way into the top 16 of a deep 83-fencer field: Sammy (Cadet Men's Épée, 13th of 83) and Ethan (Cadet Men's Épée, 16th of 83).

Results like these show that New Brunswick fencers can compete with the best young épéeists in North America. Well done, Sammy, Ethan and Bhikki!
