Hello FENB Members,

{{< host-logo id="PEIFA" >}}

The PEI Fencing Association invites you to "come and foil around" at the **PEI Foil Invitational Tournament** — one day, one weapon — on **Saturday, November 28** at the UPEI Sports Centre in Charlottetown, PE.

Read the [tournament flyer (PDF)](/documents/events/2026-27/PEI%20Foil%20Invitational%202026%20-%20Registration.pdf) for full details. Questions can go to Lothar Zimmermann at [zimmermann@upei.ca](mailto:zimmermann@upei.ca).

## Schedule

| Event | Check-in Closes | Sanctioned |
|---|---|---|
| U13 Mixed Foil | 8:45 am | No |
| U17 Mixed Foil | 10:45 am | Yes |
| Open Mixed Foil | 1:45 pm | Yes |

All fencers must have a valid CFF licence.

## Registration

There's no online form — fill in the registration form on page 2 of the [flyer](/documents/events/2026-27/PEI%20Foil%20Invitational%202026%20-%20Registration.pdf) and email it to the registrar at [peifencing@gmail.com](mailto:peifencing@gmail.com).

**[Download the registration form and pre-register by Thursday, November 26, 2026](/documents/events/2026-27/PEI%20Foil%20Invitational%202026%20-%20Registration.pdf)**

Good luck to everyone making the trip to the Island!
