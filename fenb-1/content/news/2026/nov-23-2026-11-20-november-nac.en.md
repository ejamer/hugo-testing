New Brunswick fencer Sammy Singh Ranger travelled to Columbus, Ohio for USA Fencing's November North American Cup (NAC), held November 20–23, 2026 at the Greater Columbus Convention Center. Sammy competed in the Cadet Challenger Men's Épée event.

Congratulations to Sammy! A NAC is one of the biggest stages in North American fencing, drawing hundreds of athletes from clubs across the United States, Canada and beyond, and simply earning a place on the strip is an achievement. Sammy finished in the top third of a deep 136-fencer field (Cadet Challenger Men's Épée, 40th of 136), up from 43rd at the October NAC in Orlando.

Back-to-back NAC appearances like this show that New Brunswick fencers can hold their own against the best young épéeists on the continent. Sammy represented our province with skill and determination in Columbus, and the whole New Brunswick fencing community is proud of Sammy. Well done!
