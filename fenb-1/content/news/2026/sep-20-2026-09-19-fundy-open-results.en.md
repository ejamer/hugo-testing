The Fundy Open 2026 took place September 19–20 in Saint John, NB, with eleven events contested across sabre, foil, and épée for fencers from U-13 through Senior. Pool rounds for [U-13 Mixed Épée](https://www.fencingtimelive.com/events/results/787A7368AE894AEDA05F30D2C24B4ABC) and [Senior Men's Épée](https://www.fencingtimelive.com/events/results/4BB761CEA73B4334A6B17A3504393718) were held as combined groups to seed the direct elimination brackets below.
