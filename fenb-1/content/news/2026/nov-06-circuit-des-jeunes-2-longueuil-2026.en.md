Registration is now open for the **2026 Longueuil City Challenge**, the second stop on Quebec's Circuit des Jeunes. Hosted by the [Olympia Longueuil Fencing Club](https://olympialongueuil.com/), it takes place **December 12–13** at the Sports Centre of Cégep Édouard-Montpetit, 260 rue de Gentilly Est, Longueuil, QC.

Read the [event page](https://www.escrimequebec.qc.ca/id-evenement?id=UnBPRmlUK0QrMmpEWXA1NHdnNzFxZz09) for full details, including the tournament flyer.

## Schedule

| Check-in Closes | Events |
|---|---|
| 8:20 am | Women's and Men's Épée — U17 |
| 8:45 am | Women's and Men's Épée — U13 |
| 1:00 pm | Women's and Men's Épée — U15 |

| Check-in Closes | Events |
|---|---|

| 9:30 am | Women's Sabre — U13 |
| 10:00 am | Men's Sabre — U13 |
| 12:45 pm | Men's Foil — U15; Women's Sabre — U15; Women's and Men's Foil — U11 |
| 1:00 pm | Women's Foil — U15 |
| 1:30 pm | Men's Sabre — U15 |
| 2:30 pm | Women's and Men's Sabre — U11 |

Age categories are based on year of birth: U11 (2016–2018), U13 (2014–2015), U15 (2012–2013) and U17 (2010–2011). Each fencer may enter only one event per half-day session.

**[Register here by Monday, November 30, 2026](https://2mev.com/#!/events/circuit-des-jeunes-2-longueuil-2026)**

Good luck to everyone making the trip to Longueuil!
