Our 2026-2027 FENB membership and CFF license renewal is now available!

There are 3 tiers of membership available. All fencers, coaches and officials MUST hold one of these licences in order to participate in any fencing activities.
* **Fencing Member** - For participants in all activities, including provincial ones such as tournaments and training camps.
* **Recreational Fencing Member** - For participants in club-level activities only; can be upgraded to Fencing Member later.
* **Associate Member** - For non-fencers: coaches, referees, volunteers.

*Please note that all coaches are also required to purchase a coach license.*

For more information about our memberships and how to complete your purchase, see the [Individual Membership](/join/membership/) section of our website.
