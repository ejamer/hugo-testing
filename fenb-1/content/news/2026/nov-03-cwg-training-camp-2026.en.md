Hello FENB Members,

FENB is hosting a **Canada Winter Games Training Camp** on **November 21–22** at École Carrefour de l'Acadie, 515 Champlain St, Dieppe, NB.

The camp is geared toward U17/U20 fencers getting ready for the [2027 Canada Winter Games]({{< relref "programs/canada-games-2027" >}}). It will feature conditioning and physical training, weapon-specific technical and tactical training, match analysis and bouting, as well as a CWG-style team tournament. Read the [camp flyer (PDF)](/documents/events/2026-27/FENB-CWG-Training-Camp-2026-11-21-EN.pdf) for full details.

## Schedule

| Time | Saturday, November 21 | Time | Sunday, November 22 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9:30 am–12:00 pm | Session 1: Intros, warm-up, group work | 9:30 am–12:30 pm | Session 3: Team tournament |
| 12:00–1:00 pm | Lunch | 12:30–1:30 pm | Lunch |
| 1:00–3:30 pm | Session 2: Weapon-specific work | 1:30–3:30 pm | Session 4: Analysis and debrief |

**[Register here by Wednesday, November 18, 2026](https://forms.gle/c38bKY3Pt46vzHiV7)**

See you in Dieppe!
