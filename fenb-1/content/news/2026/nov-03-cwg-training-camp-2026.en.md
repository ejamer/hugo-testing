Hello FENB Members,

FENB is hosting a **Canada Winter Games Training Camp** on **November 21–22** at École Carrefour de l'Acadie, 515 Champlain St, Dieppe, NB. Participation is **by invitation only**.

The camp is geared toward U17/U20 fencers getting ready for the [2027 Canada Winter Games]({{< relref "programs/canada-games-2027" >}}). It will feature conditioning and physical training, weapon-specific technical and tactical training, match analysis and bouting, as well as a CWG-style team tournament.

## Schedule

**Saturday, November 21**

| Time | Session |
|---|---|
| 9:30 am–12:00 pm | Session 1: Intros, warm-up, group work |
| 12:00–1:00 pm | Lunch |
| 1:00–3:30 pm | Session 2: Weapon-specific work |

**Sunday, November 22**

| Time | Session |
|---|---|
| 9:30 am–12:30 pm | Session 3: Team tournament |
| 12:30–1:30 pm | Lunch |
| 1:30–3:30 pm | Session 4: Analysis and debrief |

Good luck to all the athletes preparing for the Games!
