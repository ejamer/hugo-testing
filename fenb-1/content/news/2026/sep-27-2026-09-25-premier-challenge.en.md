Two New Brunswick fencers headed south for the Premier Challenge ROC/RJCC/RYC, held September 25–27, 2026, at the Rockland Community College – Eugene Levy Field House in Suffern, New York. Both competed in Cadet Men's Épée, one of the largest events of the weekend with 124 fencers.

Against a deep field of mostly American competitors, Ethan Han led the way with a top-15 finish (15th of 124), and Harvir (Sammy) Singh Ranger also placed in the top third (40th of 124). Congratulations to both on strong results at a major USA Fencing regional event!
