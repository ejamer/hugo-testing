Hello FENB Members,

November is almost here, and registration is open for a couple of exciting tournaments.

Whether you stay in-province at Damocles' Hoplite Invitational tournament or have your sights set on the next National event in BC, November is a great time to don your whites and get some Fall fencing in!

Don't miss your chance - register now!


## Hoplite Invitational — November 7–8 (Fredericton, NB)

{{< host-logo id="DAM" >}}

The Damocles Fencing Club is hosting the 2026 Hoplite Invitational at its club, 800 Saint Marys Street, Fredericton. Fencers from across the Maritimes are welcome.

**[Register here by Thursday, November 5, 2026](https://www.damoclesfencing.ca/hoplite-invitational)**

**Saturday, November 7**

| Event | Check-in Closes | Start |
|---|---|---|

| Senior Women's Épée (Open) | 9:00 am | 9:20 am |
| U15 Mixed Épée | 1:00 pm | 1:20 pm |

**Sunday, November 8**

| Event | Check-in Closes | Start |
|---|---|---|
| Cadet Men's Épée | 9:00 am | 9:20 am |
| Cadet Women's Épée | 9:00 am | 9:20 am |

**Fees & deadlines:** $35 per event 

Please bring a signed participant agreement to the tournament.

## Canada Cup #2 — November 28–30 (Richmond, BC)

{{< host-logo id="CFF" >}}

The second Canada Cup of the season takes place in Richmond, BC with U15/Y14, Cadet, Junior and Senior events. See the [Fencing Canada event page](https://fencing.ca/event/2026-2027-canada-cup-2-richmond-bc/) for full details, including eligibility birth years, format and hotel group rates.

**[Register on 2mev](https://2mev.com/#!/events/canada-cup-2)**

**Fees & deadlines** (all deadlines 6:00 pm Pacific):

| Deadline | Fees |
|---|---|
| **Friday, October 30, 2026** | Regular: $90 per event + $50 admin fee |
| Friday, November 13, 2026 | Double event fees |
| Friday, November 20, 2026 | Triple event fees (final deadline) |

A national CFF licence validated by FENB is required. Hotel group rates are available until **November 13**. The detailed schedule will be published after the regular-fee deadline.

Good luck to everyone competing!
