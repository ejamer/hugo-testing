Flynn Stevens of Beaches Sabre Club East represented New Brunswick at the 49th Preis der Chemiestadt in Dormagen, Germany, November 6–8, 2026 — an [FIE World Cup](https://fie.org/tournaments/2027/121) that is one of the biggest international stops on the youth sabre calendar. Flynn was New Brunswick's only fencer at the tournament and competed in two events: Cadet Men's Sabre (126 fencers) and Junior Men's Sabre (245 fencers).

Fields this size, with fencers from more than 40 countries, are exactly the kind of experience that helps athletes grow. Congratulations to Flynn on taking on some of the world's best young sabre fencers.
