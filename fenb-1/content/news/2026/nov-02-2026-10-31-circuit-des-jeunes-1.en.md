Eight New Brunswick fencers from Beaches Sabre Club East, Damocles Fencing Club and Fundy Fencing Club competed at Circuit des Jeunes #1, held October 31 and November 1, 2026 in Gatineau, Quebec. Together they took part in eight events across all three weapons.

Congratulations to Flynn Stevens and Simon Jamer, who each reached the podium in sabre!

🥉 Flynn Stevens — Cadet Men's Sabre\
🥉 Simon Jamer — U-15 Men's Sabre (Division 1)

New Brunswick fencers also landed in the top half of their fields during three other events:

- Maria Cielo Bagsican — U-13 Women's Épée (5th of 14)
- Simon Jamer — Cadet Men's Sabre (8th of 27)
- Gideon Candy — Cadet Men's Épée (16th of 44)

The U-15 events opened with combined pool rounds — [U-15 Women's Épée](https://www.fencingtimelive.com/events/results/F8243427324840C9879ED5A6EC3FCEFB) and [U-15 Men's Sabre](https://www.fencingtimelive.com/events/results/41AD1AD810B74BF190165E41AD6CCC65) — that seeded fencers into Division 1 and Division 2 direct elimination brackets.

Well done to all eight fencers on a strong weekend in Gatineau!
