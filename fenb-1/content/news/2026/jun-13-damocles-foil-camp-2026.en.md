Damocles Fencing Club is hosting the 2026 Summer Foil & Leadership Camp — three days of technical foil training combined with leadership development for fencers ages 13–19.

***Updated Event Details Below***

**Camp Details**

- **Dates:** August 25–27, 2026
- **Location:** Damocles Fencing Club, Fredericton, NB
- **Ages:** 13–19 (Junior Leader Program open to ages 16+)
- **Hours:** 8:30 AM – 4:30 PM daily
- **Fee:** $195 base fee per athlete. A $75 deposit is requested at registration, with any balance due on or before the first day of camp.

**What's Included**

- *Fencing Training & Tactics* — technical and tactical foil development, mental resilience and focus
- *Catered Lunch & Snacks* — daily nutritious snacks and catered lunch included in the base fee
- *Experienced Coaching* — Joe Kinley & Isil Flynn, plus guest coaches and special workshops

**Optional: Junior Leader Program (Ages 16+)**

Athletes can opt into an added leadership stream, which includes:

- Leading warmups, drills, and activities
- Coaching and mentoring peers
- Additional leadership instruction
