New Brunswick sent ten fencers to Montréal, QC this weekend for the Sylvie Clément Memorial Canada Cup #1 — Senior National Championships, September 25–27, 2026. Results are in for seven events so far, with three more on Sunday before the tournament wraps.

No one has reached the podium yet, but several NB fencers posted strong results through Saturday:

- STEVENS Flynn — Cadet Men's Saber (5th of 54)
- LAROCQUE Olivier — U-15 Men's Épée (9th of 31)
- STEVENS Ashelin — Junior Women's Saber (21st of 57)
- WEED Sophie — Cadet Women's Épée (24th of 52)
- SINGH RANGER Bhikki — Cadet Men's Épée (25th of 66)
- FOURNET Sebastien — Senior Men's Épée (26th of 113)
- SINGH RANGER Sammy — Cadet Men's Épée (28th of 66)
- STEVENS Flynn — Junior Men's Saber (29th of 68)
