New Brunswick sent ten fencers to Montréal, QC this weekend for the Sylvie Clément Memorial Canada Cup #1 — Senior National Championships, September 25–27, 2026. NB fencers competed in ten events over the three days.

No NB fencers reached the podium, but several posted strong results:

- STEVENS Flynn — Cadet Men's Sabre (5th of 54)
- LAROCQUE Olivier — U-15 Men's Épée (9th of 31)
- STEVENS Ashelin — Junior Women's Sabre (21st of 57)
- WEED Sophie — Cadet Women's Épée (24th of 52)
- SINGH RANGER Bhikki — Cadet Men's Épée (25th of 66)
- FOURNET Sebastien — Senior Men's Épée (26th of 113)
- SINGH RANGER Sammy — Cadet Men's Épée (28th of 66)
- STEVENS Flynn — Junior Men's Sabre (29th of 68)

Congratulations to every fencer who made the trip — we are proud of all the New Brunswick athletes who represented our province at this national-level event.
