FENB is inviting coaches to two workshops at Club d'escrime La Resistance in Moncton, offering hands-on training and 3 NCCP professional development points.

**Workshop Details**

- **Dates:** August 8–9, 2026
- **Location:** Club d'escrime La Resistance, 123 Halifax St., Moncton, NB
- **Schedule:** 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM daily (lunch: noon – 1:00 PM)
- **Fee:** Free for FENB members; $100 for out-of-province coaches
- **NCCP:** 3 professional development points

**Workshop #1 — Saturday, August 8** *(Beginner & Introductory Programs)*
: A variety of partner drills for group settings, including footwork and bladework. Participants will practice presenting basic drills and providing feedback in a group setting.

**Workshop #2 — Sunday, August 9** *(Competitive Fencers)*
: Individual lesson work for the early part of the season, with an emphasis on guiding student success and ensuring proper coordination and timing of actions.

**Mentor Coaches:** Jim Stevens, Marc-André LeBlanc, Barbara Daniel

Read the full [workshop invitation (PDF)](/documents/events/2025-26/fenb_coaching_workshop-aug2026_en.pdf) for complete details. 

Questions? Contact workshop coordinator Barbara Daniel at [bjdaniel@nbnet.nb.ca](mailto:bjdaniel@nbnet.nb.ca).
