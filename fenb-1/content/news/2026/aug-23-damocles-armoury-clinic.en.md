Damocles Fencing Club is hosting a blade wiring armoury clinic for foil and épée, led by armourer Ed Matchett.

**Clinic Details**

- **Date:** Wednesday, August 26, 2026
- **Time:** 1:30 PM – 3:30 PM
- **Location:** Damocles Fencing Club, Fredericton, NB
- **Fee:** $10.00

**What to bring (if you have them)**

- Personal armoury tools
- Blades to be re-wired
- Flexible glue that bonds to metal

Ed will cover trouble-shooting advice to determine what repairs are needed, and guide participants through the steps to rewire foil and épée blades.

Questions? Contact Barbara Daniel at [bjdaniel@nbnet.nb.ca](mailto:bjdaniel@nbnet.nb.ca) or 506-454-7886.
