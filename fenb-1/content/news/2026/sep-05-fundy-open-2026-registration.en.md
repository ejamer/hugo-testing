Hello FENB Members,

We are pleased to announce the first NB tournament of the season, the 2026 Fundy Open, happening in Saint John on September 19–20 at Harbour View High School (305 Douglas Ave.).

**Schedule:**
- **Saturday, September 19:** Cadet and U13/U15 divisions in sabre, épée, and foil; plus Open sabre. Check-in from 9:00 am to 2:00 pm.
- **Sunday, September 20:** Cadet épée, Open foil and épée. Check-in from 8:30 am, with final event check-in at 12:30 pm.

**Fees & deadlines:** $40 per event, payable by e-transfer or cash. Registration closes **Thursday, September 17, 2026**. Participants must hold a current FENB membership or valid CFF licence.

View the [event flyer](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Sk3KRkRMvHuvHp5qStZF93Om--giqMs9AsmEnrWind4/edit?usp=sharing) for full details, and [register here](https://prettyform.addxt.com/a/form/vf/1FAIpQLSeBWHmVLGI72WkVppXx9jtRfLvITFf6YWnerQoogovJsynF_A).
