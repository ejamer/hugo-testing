Hello FENB Members,

The [Halifax Enguardians Fencing Club](https://www.enguardians.ca/) has extended an invitation to all Maritime fencers to compete at the **2026 Tri-Sword Tournament**, taking place **November 14–15** at West Bedford High School, 50 Broad Street, Bedford, NS.

With U17 and Open events in épée, foil and sabre, there's something for every New Brunswick fencer — and it's just a short drive away. Read the [tournament flyer (PDF)](/documents/events/2026-27/Tri-Sword%20Tournament%202026%20-%20Registration.pdf) for full details.

## Schedule

**Saturday, November 14**

| Event | Birth Years | Check-in Closes |
|---|---|---|
| U17 Épée | 2010–2015 | 9:00 am |
| Open Foil | All | 12:00 pm |
| U17 Sabre | 2010–2015 | 2:00 pm |

**Sunday, November 15**

| Event | Birth Years | Check-in Closes |
|---|---|---|
| Open Épée | All | 9:00 am |
| U17 Foil | 2010–2015 | 12:30 pm |
| Open Sabre | All | 2:00 pm |

Age categories are as of December 31, 2026. Events are not sanctioned. You can only fence one event at a time — if two of your events overlap, you will need to choose one.

**[Register here by Wednesday, November 11, 2026](https://us.list-manage.com/GBA2t17hhPA?e=eeae2e0430&c2id=b1384f35c909fc0e3bd5aaa0361cf8bb)**

Good luck to everyone making the trip to Bedford!
